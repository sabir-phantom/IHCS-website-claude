Welcome to Iqbal Catering

A trusted name in Bangladesh's catering industry.

Established more than 35 years ago, we are known for authentic flavours, fresh ingredients and personal hospitality.

We create customized catering for weddings, corporate events, private gatherings and religious occasions. Our goal is simple: deliver quality cuisine and personal service that earns the trust of every family and company we serve.

What we cater

Four kinds of occasion, one standard.

  • Weddings & Grand Celebrations

    Traditional menus and graceful service for holud, biye and walima.

  • Corporate Events & AGM Programs

    Punctual, organized catering for conferences, meetings and launches.

  • Private & Family Gatherings

    Birthdays, anniversaries, aqiqah and reunions, at home or in a hall.

  • Outdoor & Religious Events

    Picnics, garden functions, Iftar and community programs.

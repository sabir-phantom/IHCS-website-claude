    Before you book

    What families ask us first.

    Still unsure? Call the hotline at 01713 334040.

    Will there be enough food for every guest?

    Yes. Portions are planned against your confirmed guest count before cooking starts, so the last table is served the same as the first.

    What is the minimum order?

    30 persons. Above that, we cater everything from home gatherings to full convention hall weddings.

    How much does it cost?

    Prices are shared through a quotation. It depends on the menu, guest count, event date, venue and service style.

    Can we change dishes in a set menu?

    Yes. The four set menus are starting points, and every menu can be fully customized around your budget, season and preferences.

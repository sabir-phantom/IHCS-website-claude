Our story

It started at a variety store in Mohammadpur.

Before the convention halls, there was the Iqbal Hossain Variety Store at Town Hall, Mohammadpur.

Mohd. Iqbal Hossain saw a gap: families needed a caterer they could trust with their biggest days. Iqbal Catering was his answer. Since 1987 it has served weddings, corporate programs and family dawats across Bangladesh, with the same recipes and the same care.

Today the family runs it together, and the second generation has joined the kitchen and the planning table.

      Before you book

      What families ask us first.

      Still unsure? Call the hotline at 01713 334040.

      Will there be enough food for every guest?

      Yes. Portions are planned against your confirmed guest count before cooking starts, so the last table is served the same as the first. Quantity you can count on is the promise our name carries.

      What is the minimum order?

      30 persons. Above that, we cater everything from home gatherings to full convention hall weddings.

      How much does it cost?

      Prices are shared through a quotation. It depends on the menu you choose, your guest count, the venue and the service format. Use Build your dawat below and our team will reply with a price.

      Can we change dishes in a set menu?

      Yes. The four set menus are starting points. Every menu can be fully customized around your budget, season and preferences.

      Have you worked at our venue?

      Likely. We have catered at 20+ prestigious Dhaka venues, including DICC, Sena Kunja, RAOWA Convention Hall and Army Golf Garden. Tell us the venue and we will plan around its layout.

      Do you cater outdoors or outside Dhaka?

      Yes. We provide outdoor catering for picnics, garden functions and remote venues, and we serve events across Bangladesh.
